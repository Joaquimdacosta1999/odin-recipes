Loaded Baked Potato French Toast

Loaded baked potato French toast is the most delicious mash-up I have ever created. Mashed potatoes, Cheddar cheese, green onions, crispy bacon, and sour cream bring all the flavors of a classic loaded baked potato to savory French toast, served with a spicy, sweet, and sour maple syrup.

Ingredients

Filling

  • 2 medium russet potatoes
  • 1 teaspoon Kosher salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
  • 1 pinch cayenne
  • 2 ounces Cheddar cheese, grated
  • 1/4 cup sliced green onions, plus more as needed for garnish
  • 1/2 cup crispy fried bacon bits
  • 1/4 cup sour cream

French Toast:

  • 5 large eggs
  • 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 12 slices white sandwich bread
  • 4 tablespoons unsalted butter

Spicy, Sweet and Sour Maple Syrup:

  • 1 cup maple syrup
  • 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar
  • 1 1/2 teaspoons cayenne pepper, or to taste

Directions

Step 1

Preheat the oven to 400 degrees F (200 degrees C). Line a baking sheet with aluminum foil.

Step 2

Poke potatoes with the tip of a knife and place on a the prepared baking sheet.

Step 3

Roast in the preheated oven until potatoes are very tender, 60 to 75 minutes. Let rest until cool enough to handle, then scoop out the inside of the potato while still warm into a bowl.

Stap 4

Break up potatoes into small pieces with a fork. Add salt, black pepper, cayenne, Cheddar cheese, green onions, bacon, and sour cream. Mix until everything is evenly combined for the filling, and set aside.

Step 5

Combine eggs and salt in a shallow dish. Beat with a fork until egg whites are blended in. Pour in milk and mix to combine.

Step 6

Dip each slice of bread into the egg mixture, flipping over 2 to 3 times to thoroughly coat. Divide the filling mixture evenly across 6 slices of bread. Spread filling in an even layer up to the edges. Top with remaining 6 slices of bread, and press down gently. Refrigerate for 10 to 15 minutes while you make the syrup.

Step 7

Combine maple syrup, apple cider vinegar, and cayenne in a saucepan and bring to a boil over medium-high heat. Boil for 30 seconds, stir, then turn off heat, and let cool.

Step 8

Melt butter in a large ovenproof skillet over medium heat. Brown French toast until lightly golden brown, about 3 minutes. Flip over and cook the other side for just 30 seconds before turning off heat.

Step 9

Turn down oven to 375 degrees F (190 degrees C).

Step 10

Move skillet to the hot oven and bake French toasts until filling is heated all the way through, about 15 minutes.

Step 11

Serve with the spicy, sweet and sour maple syrup. Garnish with sliced green onions.
